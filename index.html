I’m an AI research engineer at LaunchPulse and an independent researcher focused on LLM post-training, reinforcement learning, and evaluation.

I study how language models learn from feedback, how reward design shapes their behaviour, and whether training improvements translate into more reliable reasoning and tool use. I share reproducible experiments, open-source code, and technical writing covering both results and failure cases.

At LaunchPulse, I work on agentic AI and the software infrastructure behind it. My interests connect model training with practical questions about how agents write code, use tools, and recover from mistakes.

Previously, I worked on NLP at General Motors and co-founded Taqriry AI, an Arabic AI meeting assistant.

  • Agent Learning Order — A local-model memory experiment with 270 runs, raw transcripts, and reproducible evidence-order results.
  • AI Elo Ranker — A tournament engine for ranking model outputs with Swiss matchmaking, Elo and TrueSkill ratings, and live evaluation dashboards.
  • JevRouter — A contribution to a Jev-powered router that selects among models, tools, and subagents while applying availability, permission, risk, and confirmation policies.
